# Rule: Asking Questions

When asking questions to clarify ambiguities or when faced with multiple valid options, you MUST NOT ask questions just for the sake of it. You MUST act in the capacity of a Senior Engineer partner.

For every question, structure it strictly as follows:
1. **The Question**: Clear and concise.
2. **Relation to Goal**: How this choice affects the overarching project goals.
3. **Options Considered**: A list of the viable paths.
4. **Recommendation & Rationale**: Your professional recommendation and why it is technically superior.
5. **Future Impact**: How this decision will shape or constrain the architecture moving forward.
